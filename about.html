Meet Eric Bass.

Founder & Managing Partner, FutureCount. More than 30 years in financial services as a partner, practice leader and CFO.

Every company carries
its owner’s signature.

I started at Grant Thornton, where I spent 17 years, the last eight as a partner leading the financial services consulting practice. I went on to build and lead financial services consulting businesses at SMART, Navigant, IBM and CSC, and later led FinTech strategic advisory at McLaren Strategic Ventures.

Today I work directly with owners and CEOs as an adviser and CFO. I currently serve as CFO of an AI Platform-as-a-Service software company and as interim CFO of an IBM Gold software reseller and professional services provider.

My background in finance, information systems and international business helps me consider the economics, the systems and the people together. I ask direct questions, challenge assumptions and help owners compare practical options. Then I work alongside them to get it done.
