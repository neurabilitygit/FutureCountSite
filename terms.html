Website information

FutureCount Holdings LLC provides this website to explain its business support services and help you decide whether to start a conversation. The material is general information, not advice tailored to your circumstances. It may not address every factor relevant to a business investment, transaction, tax, benefits, or estate-planning decision.

Professional advice and service boundaries

Website content does not replace advice from your attorney, tax adviser, investment adviser, benefits specialist, or other appropriately qualified professional. References to fractional CFO, CRO, or COO support describe business services; they do not establish a professional engagement or imply an unverified license or credential. Specific legal, tax, regulated investment, valuation, and transaction needs should be addressed with the appropriate advisers.

Starting an engagement

Reading this site, emailing Eric, or booking an introductory meeting does not by itself create a client engagement or any promise to perform work. Any engagement should separately set out scope, responsibilities, fees, confidentiality, and other applicable terms in writing. Do not assume an introductory inquiry establishes attorney-client privilege or another protected professional relationship.

Decisions and outcomes

Business improvement, investment returns, tax outcomes, business valuations, and sale timing depend on many factors. The website does not guarantee a return, a particular valuation, a buyer, or a completed sale. You remain responsible for your decisions, with advice from the professionals appropriate to your situation. Any illustrative example should be identified as an illustration rather than an actual client result.

Partners and other services

A partner reference is not a guarantee of another provider’s performance. Specialist roles, fees, and any applicable engagement terms should be clarified before work begins. External services such as SimplyMeet operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Review those notices before providing information or committing to a service.

Website content and permitted use

FutureCount content and brand materials are protected by applicable intellectual-property law. You may view and share links to these pages. Contact us before reproducing substantial material for commercial use or presenting the FutureCount name as an endorsement. Third-party fonts and materials remain subject to their respective licenses.

Responsible use

Do not attempt unauthorized access, interfere with the website or its providers, send malicious material, or use the contact channels for unlawful activity. Avoid submitting sensitive personal or business records through an introductory email or booking form.

Accuracy availability and contact

Information and availability can change. If you notice an error or a misleading statement, use the contact page. These website notices do not replace any separately agreed service contract or limit rights that applicable law does not permit to be limited.
