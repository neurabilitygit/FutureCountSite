Privacy Policy and Cookie Notice

This page explains what information FutureCount collects through this site, how it is used, and how you can manage analytics and third-party scheduling preferences.

Who we are

FutureCount LLC operates this website at futurecountllc.com. Questions about this policy or privacy requests may be sent through the contact page on this site.

Information we collect

FutureCount may collect information you choose to provide through scheduling or communications, along with technical information associated with your visit such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, referring pages, and approximate location derived from IP address.

How we use information

We use information to operate the website, respond to inquiries, support scheduling requests, understand site performance, protect against misuse, and maintain accurate business records.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses strictly necessary technologies to support core site functionality and save your privacy choices. Optional analytics technologies are used only if you opt in. The scheduling widget on the contact page is hosted by SimplyMeet and is blocked until you allow external scheduling content.

Third parties

When enabled by you, Google Analytics processes usage data to provide aggregated measurement and reporting. The SimplyMeet scheduling widget may process information needed to display availability and complete bookings. Those third parties process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

How to manage choices

You can accept, reject, or customize optional technologies using the cookie banner when you first visit the site. After that, you can reopen your settings at any time by selecting Cookie Preferences in the footer.

Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, FutureCount honors that signal by keeping analytics disabled by default for that browser session and device profile.

Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, correct information, or object to certain processing. Where consent is required for optional technologies, you may withdraw that consent at any time through Cookie Preferences.

Retention

FutureCount keeps information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

Policy updates

FutureCount may revise this policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, service providers, or legal requirements. Material updates will be posted on this page.
