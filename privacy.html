Privacy notice

How this website and its inquiry links handle information.

Updated November 4, 2026

Who this notice covers

This notice describes the FutureCount informational website and inquiries made through its scheduling links. FutureCount is operated by FutureCount Holdings LLC. Use the contact page to request a conversation with Eric Bass about privacy.

Information involved

  • Contact information: your name, email address, business details, and the information you choose to include when you request a meeting or otherwise contact FutureCount.
  • Scheduling information: the meeting time, timezone, booking answers, and related calendar and confirmation information when you use SimplyMeet.
  • Technical information: the site host may process your IP address, requested page, browser or device information, and request time to deliver and protect the website.

This site has no account registration, payment collection, document upload, or contact form. Please limit initial inquiries to the information needed to discuss your request.

Why information is used

Inquiry and scheduling information is used to respond to you, arrange and manage meetings, understand a possible engagement, and maintain related business correspondence. Technical information supports delivery, reliability, and security of the website. A later client engagement may require additional information and separate arrangements.

Service providers and sharing

SimplyMeet processes information when you follow the booking link. Meeting details may also be handled by the connected email and calendar services used to arrange the appointment. FutureCount uses Google Workspace for business email and calendars. These services handle information under their terms and privacy notices. Information may need to be disclosed where required by law or to address security incidents. A specialist introduction does not authorize unrestricted access to your confidential business records; information-sharing arrangements should be agreed for the work.

Relevant provider notices: SimplyMeet and Google. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub’s handling of technical information is described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Cookies analytics and advertising

This website’s pages do not set cookies or use local storage, analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or embedded scheduling or social-media widgets. Manrope is served with the site rather than requested from Google Fonts. No advertising-data sale or sharing is implemented by these pages. Your browser contacts SimplyMeet only when you choose its link; that separate service may use cookies or other technologies under its own policy. If tracking tools or embeds are introduced later, this notice and any required choices must be updated before they are enabled.

Retention and security

The website does not store submitted inquiry records because it has no submission form. Email, booking, calendar, and host records are held in the relevant services. Retention of business correspondence depends on the purpose of the interaction, any subsequent engagement, applicable recordkeeping duties, and provider settings. Contact us about records associated with an inquiry. No website or email system can guarantee absolute security; avoid sending sensitive records through an introductory email or booking field.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without scheduling a meeting. To request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information, or to raise another privacy concern, use the contact page. Please identify the relevant interaction without sending sensitive identity documents. We may need to confirm your identity and the scope of the request. The rights available, exceptions, and response requirements depend on the law that applies. Requests about a third party’s independent processing may also need to go to that provider. A calendar cancellation does not itself delete every related email or provider record.

Location and third party processing

Email, calendar, scheduling, and hosting providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their notices describe their processing and applicable safeguards. This website is intended for business owners and professional contacts; it is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through an inquiry.

Changes and contact

Material changes to this site’s information practices should be reflected in this notice with an updated date. For questions, privacy requests, or concerns, use the contact page.
