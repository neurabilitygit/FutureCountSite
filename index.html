Why I created
FutureCount.

I created FutureCount to help owners of small and midsize businesses strengthen their companies and achieve a successful ownership transition or sale to a strategic buyer.

After more than 25 years working with entrepreneurs and family business owners, I remain drawn to this work: understanding what makes a company valuable, helping its owner make informed choices, and working alongside them to carry those choices through.

Decisions business owners
are weighing today.

These choices influence one another. They deserve a view of the whole business and the future you want for it.

  • Growth and investment

    A promising growth opportunity can also require more cash, people and management attention. The decision rests on how those demands fit together.

  • People and leadership

    Developing leaders, retaining key people and funding competitive pay and benefits affect your role, your company’s culture and the standards your customers expect.

  • Technology and AI

    New tools bring choices about productivity, cost and security. The useful starting point is the work you want to improve and the investment it deserves.

  • Ownership and personal wealth

    Your timing, executive incentives, family goals, tax position and a buyer’s terms all shape what a transition means for you. Continued family ownership may also be part of the plan.

How I work
with you.

First, I get to know your company and what you want to achieve. A Business Diagnostic gives us a shared understanding of performance, people, operations and systems.

We compare practical options and agree on a custom strategy. I weigh investment choices against their likely financial benefit, their effect on quality and people, and your ability to carry them through.

I then work alongside you and your team to put the plan into action, coordinate specialists and review progress.
