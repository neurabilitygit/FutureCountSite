Our approach

FutureCount aims to make this informational website usable by people with different abilities and assistive technologies. Accessibility is an ongoing design and testing activity. This statement is not a claim of certification or a guarantee that every combination of browser and assistive technology works without difficulty.

Features included

The site includes descriptive page titles and headings, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, a skip-to-content link, text alternatives for informative images, responsive layouts, and support for reduced-motion preferences. Navigation identifies the current page. Content does not rely on color alone to explain an action.

External scheduling

SimplyMeet opens as a separate service and controls its own booking interface. If you encounter difficulty with the booking page, try the options available through SimplyMeet support. Third-party tools may have accessibility limitations outside FutureCount’s direct control.

Report a barrier

Use the booking link on the contact page to request a conversation about a website barrier. During the conversation, describe the difficulty you encountered. Include the page address and the task you were trying to complete. If useful, include your browser and assistive technology. Do not include private medical information. Tell us if you need the information in another accessible format; we will discuss an appropriate alternative.
